CITY GUIDE · MIAMI

Miami without
wasting the weekend.

Miami is easier when you stop treating it like one giant neighborhood. This guide is built around clusters so you spend less time in traffic and more time actually enjoying the city.

8.8

WORTH YOUR TIME.
Best for a first visit when you want a mix of food, design, culture, coffee and nightlife rather than an all-beach itinerary.

01 · Pick your Miami

South Beach is the postcard version: architecture, beach access and late nights. Wynwood / Design District is better for galleries, shopping, restaurants and a more design-heavy day. Little Havana is the move for Cuban food, coffee and neighborhood energy. Brickell works when convenience, rooftops and a polished dinner matter more than charm.

South Beach + Little Havana

02 · A better 48-hour plan

Friday: arrive + South Beach

Check in, walk the Art Deco district near sunset, then choose one serious dinner instead of trying to hit three places. Save your late-night energy for the neighborhood you actually want to explore.

Saturday: Little Havana → Design District → night out

Start with Cuban coffee and breakfast in Little Havana. Spend the afternoon around the Design District and nearby galleries. Return to your hotel before dinner so the night feels intentional rather than like a race across town.

Sunday: slow morning + one last stop

Do not overbook your final day. Pick a good café, take a walk, then leave room for one last meal or shop before heading home.

THE WORTH-IT RULE: Miami traffic is a hidden cost. A slightly more expensive option that saves 45 minutes can be the better deal.

03 · What to skip

  • Trying to cross the entire city for one hyped restaurant.
  • Planning every hour of a beach day.
  • Assuming the most expensive rooftop automatically has the best experience.
  • Booking a tour without checking how much time it spends in transit.
