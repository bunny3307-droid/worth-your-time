Why Miami

Miami rewards people who plan around neighborhoods instead of trying to see the whole city in one trip. It is really several distinct cities pushed together—Cuban Miami, design Miami, beach Miami, glass-tower Miami—connected by roads that get slow at exactly the times you want to move. Once you accept that and build your days around one or two areas at a time, it becomes one of the easiest cities in the country to have a genuinely good weekend in.

GET AROUND

Rideshare or a car; skip driving at rush hour

What to skip

  • Crossing the entire city for one hyped restaurant.
  • Planning every hour of a beach day.
  • Assuming the most expensive rooftop automatically has the best experience.
  • Booking a tour without checking how much time it spends in transit.
  • Renting a car if your whole trip is South Beach and Brickell—rideshare is usually cheaper once you count parking.
THE WORTH-IT RULE: Miami traffic is a hidden cost. A slightly more expensive option that saves significant travel time can be the better deal.
