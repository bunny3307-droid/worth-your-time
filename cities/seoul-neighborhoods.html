SEOUL · NEIGHBORHOODS

Which part of Seoul actually fits your trip.

Seoul rewards travelers who plan by neighborhood cluster. Here is what each one is actually good for.

Seoul is dense and well connected, which makes it tempting to treat the whole city as one giant list. The better approach: understand what each neighborhood is actually for, then build your days around two or three of them at a time.

Jongno & Insadong

Historic Seoul: Gyeongbokgung Palace, traditional hanok streets and some of the city's oldest markets. This is the best area for culture and history, and a strong first stop on any first trip.

Bukchon Hanok Village

A hillside neighborhood of preserved traditional Korean houses, walkable from Jongno and Insadong. It is a residential area as much as an attraction, so keep voices down and stay on marked public paths.

Hongdae

Named for Hongik University, this is Seoul's youthful, indie energy: street performers, clubs, cheap and inventive food, and a shopping scene aimed at a younger crowd. Best in the afternoon through late night.

Seongsu

A former industrial district turned design neighborhood—sometimes called Seoul's answer to Brooklyn. Converted warehouses now hold some of the city's best cafés, concept stores and pop-up shops. Best for slow wandering rather than a checklist.

Gangnam

Modern, polished and shopping-forward: high-end retail, K-beauty flagship stores, and a more corporate, upscale nightlife scene. A useful contrast to the historic core, and a strong base for shopping-focused trips.

Itaewon

Seoul's most international neighborhood, with the city's widest range of non-Korean food and a nightlife scene that draws a mixed local and expat crowd. Good for variety when you want a break from Korean cuisine specifically.

Myeongdong

A dense shopping and street-food district, extremely convenient and central, but also the most tourist-heavy area in the city. A good home base for logistics, less so for an authentic sit-down meal.

Ikseon-dong

A pocket of restored hanok alleyways near Jongno turned into cafés, bars and boutiques—one of the most photogenic and currently trendiest small areas in the city.

FIRST TRIP

Jongno + Hongdae + Seongsu

FOOD-FOCUSED

Jongno + Euljiro + Mangwon

NIGHTLIFE

Hongdae + Itaewon
