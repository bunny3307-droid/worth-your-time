MIAMI · WHERE TO STAY

Matching your hotel to your itinerary.

In a spread-out city like Miami, the neighborhood you sleep in decides half of your trip. Pick it based on what you are actually there to do.

South Beach — for beach + nightlife

The obvious pick if beach access and nightlife are the priority. You will pay a premium for it, and traffic in and out during peak hours can be genuinely slow—worth it if you plan to stay local most nights.

Brickell — for convenience

The most practical base for a first-timer who wants easy access to Downtown, the Design District and the airport, with a walkable core of its own restaurants and bars. Less vacation character, more logistics working in your favor.

Wynwood / Design District — for a design-forward trip

Fewer hotel options, but the right call if food, art and shopping matter more to your trip than beach time. You will likely still rideshare to the beach rather than walk to it.

Coral Gables — for a quieter, upscale trip

A calmer, greener base with genuine architectural character, better suited to a slower trip or a family visit than a nightlife-focused weekend.

Key Biscayne — for a resort-style escape

The furthest from the main sights, but the quietest and most beach-resort-like option, well suited to a trip that is deliberately about slowing down.

FIRST TIMER

South Beach or Brickell

DESIGN & FOOD TRIP

Wynwood / Design District

SLOW TRIP

Coral Gables or Key Biscayne

THE WORTH-IT RULE: A cheaper hotel that adds thirty minutes of traffic to every single outing is not actually the cheaper option once you count the time. Weigh location as heavily as the room itself.
