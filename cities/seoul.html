CITY GUIDE · SEOUL

Seoul without turning your trip into a commute.

A first-timer-friendly Seoul planning system: neighborhood clusters, food, cafés, shopping, culture and nightlife with less unnecessary backtracking.

9.1

WORTH YOUR TIME.
Seoul rewards travelers who plan by neighborhood rather than trying to “see everything.” The city is dense, connected and full of places that become better when you give them time.

01 · Pick your clusters

Jongno / Insadong is useful for historic Seoul, palaces, traditional streets and culture. Hongdae works for youthful energy, shopping, cafés and nightlife. Seongsu is strong for design, pop-ups, cafés and a slower daytime wander. Gangnam makes sense for a polished, modern side of the city. Itaewon is useful when food variety and nightlife matter.

FIRST TRIP

Jongno + Hongdae + Seongsu

FOOD

Jongno + Euljiro + Mangwon

NIGHTLIFE

Hongdae + Itaewon

02 · A simple 4-day shape

Day 1 · Historic Seoul

Keep the first day geographically tight. Pair historic sights with nearby streets and a meal so the day feels like a neighborhood rather than a checklist.

Day 2 · Seongsu + design

Give yourself time to browse. Seongsu works best when you leave room for cafés, stores and whatever temporary thing catches your eye.

Day 3 · Hongdae + nightlife

Use the afternoon for shopping and cafés, then stay local for dinner and the night rather than commuting back and forth.

Day 4 · Gangnam or Itaewon + flexible time

Choose based on your mood and interests. Keep the final day lighter so you have room for a repeat visit to a favorite neighborhood.

THE WORTH-IT RULE: In Seoul, a “nearby” plan can beat a “best of Seoul” plan because the city is so easy to keep exploring once you are already in the right area.

03 · What to skip

  • Cross-city trips for a single café photo.
  • Booking every meal months ahead when the neighborhood has plenty of options.
  • Stacking major attractions into one day with no recovery time.
  • Assuming the most viral place is the best use of your limited hours.
