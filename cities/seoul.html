Seoul without turning your trip into a commute.

A first-timer-friendly Seoul planning system: neighborhood clusters, food, cafés, shopping, culture and nightlife with less unnecessary backtracking.

9.1

WORTH YOUR TIME.
Seoul rewards travelers who plan by neighborhood rather than trying to see everything. The city is dense, connected and full of places that get better when you give them time.

Why Seoul

Seoul is one of the easiest big cities in the world to explore once you understand its shape: a set of distinct, walkable neighborhoods connected by one of the best subway systems anywhere. The mistake most first-time visitors make is trying to cram a highlight from every neighborhood into every day. The better trip clusters by area, leaves room to wander, and treats the subway map as a planning tool rather than a maze.

BEST TIME

Spring (Mar–Apr) or fall (Oct–Nov)

GET AROUND

T-money card + subway, almost everywhere

HOME BASE

Myeongdong or Jongno for a first trip

What to skip

  • Cross-city trips for a single café photo.
  • Booking every meal months ahead when the neighborhood has plenty of options.
  • Stacking major attractions into one day with no recovery time.
  • Assuming the most viral place is the best use of your limited hours.
  • Missing the last subway—know your route home before the night starts.
THE WORTH-IT RULE: In Seoul, a nearby plan can beat a best-of-Seoul plan, because the city is so easy to keep exploring once you are already in the right area.
