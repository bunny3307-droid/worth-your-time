SEOUL · NIGHTLIFE

From Hongdae clubs to pojangmacha tents.

Seoul nightlife covers a wide range—know which scene you actually want, and know how you are getting home.

Hongdae: clubs and live music

The center of Seoul's club and live-music scene, driven by the university crowd nearby. Busiest Friday and Saturday nights, with street performances often starting earlier in the evening.

Itaewon: international bars

The most internationally diverse nightlife in the city, with a wide range of bars and a mixed local and expat crowd. A good pick when you want variety over a single specific scene.

Gangnam: upscale clubs and noraebang

A more polished, higher-spend club scene, plus noraebang (private karaoke rooms)—a genuinely fun, low-pressure way to spend a night out with a group, and one of the most distinctly Korean nightlife traditions.

Jongno & Euljiro: pojangmacha and hof culture

Pojangmacha are orange tent bars serving late-night food and soju; hofs are casual beer-and-snacks bars found on nearly every block. This is the most affordable, most local version of a night out in Seoul.

LOGISTICS: Seoul's subway generally stops running around midnight and restarts around 5:30am. If you are staying out late, plan for a taxi, or use the late-night 'Owl Bus' network, which runs specific routes overnight.
