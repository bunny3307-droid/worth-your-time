SEOUL · WHERE TO STAY

Matching your base to your itinerary.

Seoul's subway makes almost any neighborhood workable, but the right base still saves you real time. Here is how to pick it.

Myeongdong — for first-timer convenience

Central, well connected and extremely convenient, with shopping and street food right outside your door. The tradeoff is crowds and a more tourist-heavy feel than other options.

Hongdae — for youth, nightlife and budget

A strong pick for nightlife-focused trips and budget-conscious travelers, with good transit access and a young, energetic neighborhood outside your hotel.

Gangnam — for modern comfort and shopping

Polished, modern hotel stock and easy access to shopping and business districts—less historic character, more convenience and comfort.

Jongno & Insadong — for culture and history

The best base for a culture-first trip: palaces, hanok villages and traditional markets within walking distance, with a more local, less flashy feel than Gangnam or Myeongdong.

Itaewon — for international travelers

The most English-friendly, internationally diverse neighborhood, with a wide range of dining options—a comfortable choice for a first international trip specifically.

FIRST TIMER

Myeongdong or Jongno

NIGHTLIFE-FOCUSED

Hongdae

BUSINESS & COMFORT

Gangnam

THE WORTH-IT RULE: In Seoul, proximity to a subway line matters more than proximity to any single attraction—almost everywhere is twenty to thirty minutes from almost everywhere else once you are on the right line.
