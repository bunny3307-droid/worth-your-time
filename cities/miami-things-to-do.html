MIAMI · THINGS TO DO

Museums, art and culture beyond the beach.

Miami has a real cultural scene once you look past the beach postcard. Here is what is actually worth the time.

Wynwood Walls

An outdoor museum of large-scale murals from artists around the world, spread across a few blocks of what used to be a warehouse district. Free to walk around the surrounding streets; the core Wynwood Walls venue has a modest entry fee. Budget an hour or two, and pair it with lunch at a nearby food hall.

Pérez Art Museum Miami (PAMM)

A waterfront contemporary art museum on Biscayne Bay with rotating exhibitions and a genuinely striking building. A good rainy-day or midday option, walkable from Downtown.

Vizcaya Museum & Gardens

Covered in the beaches guide too, because it works as both: an early-1900s estate with European-style architecture and formal gardens on the bay. One of the most complete, one-stop attractions in the city.

Calle Ocho & Domino Park

The heart of Little Havana: cigar shops, live music, and Domino Park (officially Maximo Gomez Park), where locals have played dominoes for decades. Walk it slowly, mid-afternoon into evening, rather than rushing through.

Lincoln Road

A pedestrian-only shopping and dining promenade in South Beach—good for an easy evening walk, people-watching and a meal, without needing a car.

Boat and bay tours

From Bayside Marketplace and elsewhere, short boat tours run past the mansions of Star Island and out into Biscayne Bay—an easy way to see the city's waterfront from a different angle.

HALF DAY

Wynwood Walls + a food hall

RAINY DAY

PAMM or Vizcaya

EVENING

Lincoln Road walk + dinner
