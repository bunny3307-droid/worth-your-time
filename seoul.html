CITY GUIDE · SEOUL

Seoul, but
make it efficient.

The easiest way to waste Seoul is to plan it as a checklist. Build your days around neighborhoods, then leave room for food, cafés, shopping and the unexpected.

9.1

WORTH YOUR TIME.
Exceptional for food, cafés, shopping, nightlife and neighborhood wandering. The main challenge is simply deciding what not to do.

01 · Build around neighborhoods

Keep each day geographically tight. Hongdae is strong for youthful nightlife, cafés and shopping. Seongsu is ideal for design, pop-ups and cafés. Insadong / Bukchon gives you history and traditional streets. Gangnam is useful for beauty, shopping and a polished city feel. Itaewon is the better choice when you want international food and nightlife.

02 · The rule that saves the most time

Do not schedule a café, restaurant or attraction on the opposite side of the city because it went viral. Seoul rewards clusters. A neighborhood with five good options beats one “must-do” that costs you two hours of transit.

FIRST TIMER

Bukchon + Insadong + Myeongdong

YOUNG / CREATIVE

Seongsu + Hongdae

NIGHTLIFE

Hongdae + Itaewon

03 · What deserves booking

Book anything with limited capacity, a fixed time or unusually high demand. For flexible cafés, markets and neighborhood walks, keep the schedule loose. The point of a Seoul trip is not to prove you followed a spreadsheet.

THE WORTH-IT RULE: If an activity is far away, ask what else you can do nearby. A good itinerary compounds each hour.

04 · A simple 4-day shape

  • Day 1: Historic Seoul + food.
  • Day 2: Seongsu + design + cafés.
  • Day 3: Hongdae + nightlife.
  • Day 4: Gangnam or Itaewon + flexible time.
