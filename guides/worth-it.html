DECISION GUIDE

The Worth-It Test.

Price is only one part of value. We judge things by what they give back relative to the money, effort, time and alternatives.

5×

OUR FIVE QUESTIONS.
Would we spend the money? The time? Would we recommend it to a friend? And would we still recommend it if we were not getting paid?

01 · Quality

Does the actual experience deliver? A beautiful room with terrible service is not a 10. A cheap meal that tastes twice as good as expected may be.

02 · Cost

We consider the total price, not just the headline number. Fees, transportation, tips, booking friction and opportunity cost all count.

03 · Time

Your Saturday afternoon is valuable. If something takes three hours, it needs to justify three hours. Convenience can be part of value.

04 · Alternatives

The right question is rarely “is this good?” It is “is this the best use of my money and time compared with the other options?”

05 · Repeatability

Would we do it again? Some experiences are worth doing once. Others become reliable recommendations you can return to.

THE FINAL TEST: Would we recommend it to a friend who had to pay full price?
