EXPERIENCE GUIDE

When a tour is
actually worth it.

A tour should buy you something you cannot easily create yourself: access, expertise, convenience, context or a better use of time.

Pay for access

Limited-entry sites, special experiences and complicated logistics are where organized tours can earn their price.

Pay for expertise

A great guide changes what you notice. History, food and cultural experiences can be dramatically better when someone can explain what you are seeing.

Pay to eliminate friction

If transportation, reservations and coordination would consume half your day, the tour can be the better deal even when it looks expensive.

DIY when flexibility matters

If the destination is easy to navigate and your priorities are flexible, build your own day. You will often save money and gain freedom.

QUICK RULE: If you can reproduce the experience with a transit card, Google Maps and a reservation in ten minutes, you probably do not need the tour.
