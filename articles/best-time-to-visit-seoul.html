TRAVEL · SEASONAL GUIDE

The Best Time to Visit Seoul.

Seoul has four distinct seasons, and two of them are considerably better for a first trip than the other two.

Spring (March–May): cherry blossoms

Widely considered the best season to visit—mild temperatures and the famous cherry blossom bloom, typically peaking in early April, though the exact date shifts year to year. This is also peak tourist season for exactly that reason; book accommodations early if your trip lands in late March or early April.

Summer (June–August): heat and monsoon

Hot, humid, and includes Korea's monsoon season—usually concentrated in July—with heavy, sometimes prolonged rain. Not the best season for a first trip focused on walking and outdoor sightseeing, though indoor culture (museums, cafés, shopping) still works well.

Fall (September–November): foliage

The other top-tier season—cooler, dry, and known for fall foliage that typically peaks in late October to early November, especially in and around the city's palaces and mountains. Comfortable walking weather with fewer crowds than spring.

Winter (December–February): cold and dry

Cold, sometimes well below freezing, but dry and clear—good for indoor culture, café culture and markets, less ideal for long outdoor days. Prices are typically lower, and the cold is manageable with proper layering.

BEST FOR FIRST-TIMERS

Spring or fall

BEST VALUE

Winter

PLAN AROUND

July monsoon season

THE WORTH-IT RULE: If cherry blossoms or fall foliage specifically are the goal, book your accommodation as early as possible—both windows are short, weather-dependent and heavily in demand.
