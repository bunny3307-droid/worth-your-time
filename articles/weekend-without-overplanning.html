DO · PLANNING GUIDE

How to Plan a Weekend Without Overplanning It

Use anchors, clusters and empty space to make a weekend feel full without making it feel scheduled.

01 · Pick three anchors

Choose the three things you would be disappointed to miss. Everything else is supporting material. This prevents the common mistake of filling every hour with things that are merely available.

02 · Cluster the map

Give each half-day a geographic center. A coffee shop, museum, restaurant and bar that are close together can create a richer day than four “best” places spread across a city.

03 · Leave a blank block

Keep at least one block open. You can use it for a recommendation from someone you meet, a longer meal, shopping you did not expect to enjoy or simply resting.

04 · Protect the best part of the day

Do not spend your most energetic hours on logistics. If nightlife is the point of the trip, build the day so you are not exhausted before dinner.

THE RULE: A good itinerary tells you where to be. A great itinerary also gives you permission to stay longer.
