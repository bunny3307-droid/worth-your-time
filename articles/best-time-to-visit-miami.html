TRAVEL · SEASONAL GUIDE

The Best Time to Visit Miami.

Miami's weather swings hard between seasons. Here is what each one actually means for your trip, not just the average temperature.

December through April: the dry season

This is Miami's high season for a reason—warm, dry, low-humidity days and the best conditions for beach time, outdoor dining and walking around without melting. It is also the most expensive and most crowded stretch, especially around the holidays and Art Basel, the major international art fair held each December in Miami Beach.

May through November: hurricane season

Officially June through November, with the real risk concentrated August through October. Most days are unaffected, but afternoon thunderstorms are common, humidity climbs, and there is a real chance—not a guarantee—of a storm disrupting travel plans. Flight and hotel prices drop noticeably in exchange.

The shoulder-season case

Late April to May and November are a reasonable middle ground: fewer crowds than peak winter, lower prices than the depths of summer, and a meaningfully lower storm risk than late summer specifically.

BEST WEATHER

January–March

BEST VALUE

May or November

HIGHEST STORM RISK

August–October

THE WORTH-IT RULE: If your trip is flexible, shoulder season gets you most of the dry-season weather at a noticeably lower price. If your dates are fixed for summer or early fall, buy travel insurance and keep an eye on the forecast in the week before you fly.
