ABOUT THE PUBLICATION

Curation is the product.

Worth Your Time exists for people who are tired of researching everything from scratch. We publish practical recommendations for travel, food, experiences, products and places—with enough context to make the decision easier.

Our standard

Every recommendation should have a reason. We look at quality, price, convenience, atmosphere, uniqueness and the opportunity cost of spending your time there.

Our verdicts

WORTH YOUR TIME means the experience clears our threshold for quality relative to its cost and effort. NOT WORTH IT means the alternatives are better, the price is inflated, or the experience does not justify the time.

How we make money

The publication may use advertising, affiliate links, sponsored placements and partnerships. Those relationships will be disclosed, and commercial compensation will not determine an editorial verdict.
